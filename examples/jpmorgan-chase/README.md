# JPMorgan Chase Q2 2026 case study

This independent public-data demonstration applies the Earnings to AI Strategist workflow to JPMorgan Chase's Q2 2026 reporting context.

## Recommended pilot hypothesis

A 30-day, shadow-mode pilot of an AI use-case evidence and portfolio review assistant for one non-customer-facing operations portfolio.

The assistant would assemble traceable evidence for a monthly human decision to advance, hold, merge, or retire selected AI use cases. It would not approve funding, deploy models, change controls, make employment decisions, or communicate with clients.

## Important limitation

The recommendation is a hypothesis derived from public materials. Public evidence does not establish JPMorgan Chase's internal registry quality, governance maturity, data accessibility, implementation effort, or ROI.
