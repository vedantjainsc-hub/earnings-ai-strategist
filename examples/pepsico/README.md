# PepsiCo Q2 2026 case study

This independent public-data demonstration applies the Earnings to AI Strategist workflow to PepsiCo's Q2 2026 reporting context.

## Recommended pilot hypothesis

A 30-day, shadow-mode pilot of AI-assisted order-fill exception prioritization in one PepsiCo Foods North America distribution workflow, subject to a five-day data, privacy, and baseline validation gate.

The tool would rank eligible exceptions and display the source fields behind each recommendation. An authorized planner would retain every operational decision. No orders, inventory allocations, customer commitments, production schedules, or transport plans would be changed by the pilot.

## Important limitation

The recommendation is a hypothesis derived from public materials. Public evidence does not establish PepsiCo's internal workflow, exception volume, data quality, implementation effort, or ROI. The memo is not affiliated with or endorsed by PepsiCo and does not claim that its current operations are deficient.
