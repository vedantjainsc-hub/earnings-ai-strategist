# Limitations and prohibited claims

## Current limitations

- Public disclosures do not reveal internal workflow details, system architecture, ownership, data quality, or operating cost.
- Management statements are attributable claims, not independent verification.
- Earnings materials may emphasize favorable interpretations and omit operational detail.
- Transcript availability and licensing differ by company.
- A public-data analysis cannot establish internal data readiness, implementation feasibility, ROI, prevalence, or causality.
- Fixed scoring weights may not represent a specific company's priorities.
- The system has not yet been benchmarked on a large human-reviewed case set.
- The current distribution is a Hermes specialist agent, not a standalone application.

## Claims the agent must not make without additional evidence

- that an observed problem is the company's most frequent or costly problem;
- that a proposed AI intervention will produce a specific financial return;
- that the company has the data, systems, permissions, or team required to implement it;
- that management's interpretation is objectively correct;
- that a ranking is robust when small input changes reverse it;
- that a pilot is production-ready;
- that a public company analyzed by the tool uses, approved, or endorsed the recommendation.

## Intended use

The agent creates an evidence-backed strategy hypothesis and validation plan. Internal discovery, security and privacy review, technical architecture, financial approval, and accountable human decision making remain necessary.
